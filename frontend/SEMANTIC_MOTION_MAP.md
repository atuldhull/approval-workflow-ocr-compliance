The interactive visual register displays at most 24 records, with an explicit cap note and full operational list below. Lightweight background diagrams show at most eight index motifs; they are supporting compositions, not a complete quantitative chart. The dossier scene renders up to the same 24 supplied records as the register. Source IDs and status strings never change during scroll.

Truth boundaries: a route position does not prove completion; the ledger entry count is not expanded into fabricated events; replay coverage remains distinct from hash continuity; sample slots/obligations are never saved; OCR comparison remains commit-before-reveal. The existing adapters, provenance helpers and backend contracts are unchanged.
